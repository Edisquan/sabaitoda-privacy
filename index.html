Privacy Policy for SABAITODA

Effective Date: November 12, 2026

SABAITODA ("we," "our," or "us") operates the SABAITODA mobile application, serving the tricycle community in Barangay Bago Gallera, Davao City. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, phone number, email address, and profile details (including driver/operator TODA credentials).
  • Location Data: Precise real-time GPS location to match passengers with nearby tricycles and calculate routes within Bago Gallera.
  • Ride & Usage Data: Trip history, pickup/drop-off points, timestamps, and app diagnostic logs.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to facilitate real-time ride-hailing and queue dispatch matching between passengers and drivers, maintain community safety, verify registered drivers, and resolve ride disputes.

3. Data Storage and Third-Party Services

We use trusted infrastructure providers—such as Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) and Google Maps Platform—to securely store data and provide mapping services. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

4. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain your personal data only as long as your account is active or needed to provide our services. You may request the deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below.
