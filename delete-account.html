Account Deletion Request - SABAITODA

Developer: Edisquan

Users of the SABAITODA mobile application (serving Barangay Bago Gallera, Davao City) may request the permanent deletion of their account and associated personal data at any time.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. In-App: Open the SABAITODA app, go to your Profile / Settings screen, and select Delete Account (if available in your app version).
  2. Via Email (Web Request): Send an email to malveze111@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request - SABAITODA" and include the registered email address or phone number associated with your account.

Data Deleted and Retention Policy

  • Data Deleted: Upon verification, we permanently delete your user profile, login credentials (Firebase Authentication), phone number, email address, and saved driver/passenger details within 30 days.
  • Data Retained: Anonymized trip logs or dispatch records without personally identifiable information may be retained for up to 90 days solely for community safety auditing, fraud prevention, and system diagnostics, after which they are permanently purged.
